## 5. Design Preferences

### Overall Design Direction

Create a premium, ambient, futuristic portfolio that feels like an interactive digital environment rather than a conventional developer template.

The website should communicate technical ability, creativity, and attention to detail.

**Theme:** Dark mode

**Color Palette:**

* Background: Near-black and deep midnight blue
* Primary accent: Electric violet
* Secondary accent: Cyan and icy blue
* Additional accent: Soft magenta
* Main text: Off-white
* Secondary text: Cool gray

**Visual Style:** Ambient, futuristic, minimal, cinematic, interactive, and polished.

### Background and Atmosphere

Create an immersive background with:

* Animated radial gradients that slowly move across the screen.
* Soft violet and blue ambient lighting.
* Subtle floating particles.
* A fine grain texture for depth.
* Gentle glow effects around interactive elements.
* Layered gradients that create a sense of space.
* A subtle animated grid or abstract technical pattern in selected sections.

The background should feel alive without distracting visitors from the content.

Avoid excessive neon, strong flashing effects, and visual clutter.

### Animation and Transition Requirements

Use animations throughout the website, with a consistent motion style.

**Page Load**

* Smooth initial entrance animation.
* Name and headline revealed through a subtle text animation.
* Background gradients gradually fade into view.
* Hero elements appear with staggered timing.

**Scroll Animations**

* Sections reveal themselves as they enter the viewport.
* Project cards animate into position.
* Skill categories appear with staggered delays.
* About and education sections use subtle parallax effects.
* Timeline elements animate progressively.

**Hover Interactions**

* Project cards lift slightly when hovered.
* Borders develop a subtle gradient glow.
* Buttons respond with smooth scaling and lighting.
* Technology badges have small, controlled hover animations.
* Navigation links use animated underlines or gradient indicators.

**Mouse Interactions**

* A subtle ambient light can follow the cursor on desktop.
* Interactive cards may use a restrained 3D tilt effect.
* Avoid excessive cursor trails or effects that interfere with usability.

**Transitions**

* Use smooth transitions between sections.
* Add subtle page transitions if routing is implemented.
* Use consistent easing curves and durations.
* Keep interactions responsive and avoid unnecessary animation delays.

**Performance Requirements**

* Prefer CSS transforms and opacity for animation.
* Use lightweight CSS or canvas effects instead of unnecessarily expensive rendering.
* Lazy-load large project images.
* Respect the user's reduced-motion preference.
* Ensure animations work smoothly on mobile devices.
